Slate

Add a projection source

Any CSV with a player name column and a points column works, including the free Daily Fantasy Fuel download (it’s recognized automatically). Players are matched to the slate by name (defenses by team). Import one file per source, then weight them below.

Sources and weights

A player’s projection is the weighted average of every source that’s ticked and has him. Untick a source to turn it off without losing its numbers; you can also tick sources on the Players tab. You can still override any single player there.

Build settings

Each lineup is the exact best score under your rules. Randomness nudges projections up or down per lineup for variety; duplicate lineups are never returned. Locked players appear in every lineup, and per-player max exposure on the Players tab overrides the global cap.
